About

Design grounded in how people actually learn

I bring more than a decade of experience designing, delivering, evaluating, and refining learning across higher education, K-12, and international settings.

My background in English rhetoric helps me make information clear, purposeful, and usable. My teaching experience gives me a direct view of where learners get stuck, how instructions fail, and how feedback can guide a better next attempt.

I am especially interested in instructional design, curriculum design, learning experience design, higher education, and education technology.

My approach

Clarity is not simplification. It is the careful design of what a learner needs to notice, decide, practice, and do next.
